The explainer — how the program works

One long video becomes dozens of short clips, posted everywhere by people getting their link out.

JRNY.CLUB

A clipper cuts one strong moment from a long video — 15 to 60 seconds, vertical, captioned, hook in the first 2 seconds — and posts it on their own TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X account.

Clipping started with gamers cutting moments from long streams. JRNY uses the same idea for launchpad videos. No follower minimum. One team cannot post 50 clips a day across dozens of accounts — clippers can.

Who pays what — read this first. JRNY pays only the affiliate % on referred trading fees. $0 per view, $0 flat fee, $0 per post, $0 per sign-up — paid by JRNY. Anything else is paid by someone else: TikTok, YouTube or Instagram pay rewards direct to the poster's own account, and other brands pay for their own campaigns. JRNY does not pay, top up, or owe those amounts.

Only people JRNY has given written permission to may cut and post JRNY videos and earn from them. Credit is not permission. Without permission, posting is copyright infringement and platforms can strike or close the account. The diagram is the whole rule:

Lifetime means lifetime

You earn a % of a referred person's trading fees for as long as that person trades — not once, not for 30 days. Put the exact % and the lifetime term in writing in the brief.

Built with Discord, Drive and a written brief. Cost to JRNY in pay: $0 — no per-view budget, no flat fees, no hired channels, all paid by JRNY at $0.

  1. Pick one goal, one call to action per cut.
  2. Make footage worth cutting — plain-English founder talk, live demo, AMA. Keep recordings public.
  3. Build the media library — see Chapter VII.
  4. Write the brief — this is the contract: what to cut / never cut · no price predictions or “you will make money” claims · don't cut context to change meaning · credit + disclosure (see Watch Out) · format 9:16, captions on, 15–60s · own link/code in bio · no bought views or bots.
  5. Set the money — only the % in How You Earn, in writing, plus when claimable.
  6. Recruit in Discord — #clips channel, pin brief, library link, terms and submission form.
  7. Review + track — check views on the platform, not screenshots; track sign-ups and trading fees per poster. Tools: Discord, Drive, Sheets/Forms, CapCut.
  • Paid cuts are ads: disclose commission in the video itself, near the start, and in the caption — “I get commissions for sign-ups through links in this post” — plus use the platform's paid-partnership toggle. Hashtags alone are not enough.
  • Fake views + cut farming: no bought views, bots or paid boosts. Warning signs: views spike then stop, generic repeated comments, wrong country, views up but sign-ups flat. JRNY can hold payment while a person reviews it.
  • Payment disputes: JRNY has no per-view payments, so no per-view disputes. The only money term is the affiliate %, in writing — reject nothing for a reason that was not in the brief first.
  • Scam warning: a real program never charges you to join, never asks for a password or phone code, and never pays only in gift cards.
  • No promises: never claim anyone will make money, and never predict a coin price.
